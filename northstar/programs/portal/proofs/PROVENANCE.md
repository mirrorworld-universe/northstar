# Does the recorded withdrawal match the call?

The [entitlement check](ENTITLEMENT.md) matches a sealed payout row to a withdrawal effect. The [V2 replay gate](../../../src/continuity_v2/token_provenance.rs) separately checks where each effect came from. It reconstructs requests from the retained transaction and compares them **in order, including duplicates**, with the token effects. It does not take the destination or amount from the proposed payout. `RecordedTransitionV2::apply` runs this gate on append, checkpoint construction and validation, and journal reopen, before publishing the candidate state.

| Evidence | What replay checks | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct `StartWithdrawal` | Solana verifies all signatures of the canonical retained message. The instruction names the holding owner in the signed prefix, the holding is writable, and the bridge, registry, mint, destination, raw amount and decimals match. | The signature authorizes the message; by itself it does not establish that the bank executed it successfully. |
| CPI `StartWithdrawal` | The signed outer instruction anchors an ordered invocation stack. Every child's account indices must be visible in its immediate parent, including across sibling returns. The withdrawal uses the owner of the matched holding. | Inner data and effective PDA signer privileges come from the successful SVM trace, **not** an independent owner signature. A structurally consistent trace is not a proof that it was executed. |
| v0 address lookup | The message signs the table keys and indices. Recording retains the table bytes and cross-checks the resolved order against the bank loader. Replay checks the table owner and bytes against a committed account or readonly observation, then resolves writable keys before readonly keys. | A retained observation is not independently authenticated L1 state. Table activation and historical bank selection remain bank obligations. |

Replay reconstructs the V2 registration from the session identity and call fields, including the token program and derived vault, then checks its account opening. A known holding cannot disappear from both the reported writes and token effects of a successful call. A failed transaction cannot issue a token effect or change a touched canonical holding. Token-deposit ingress must not introduce a withdrawal. Existing liability and cumulative-counter checks still apply.

**Compatibility:** no Portal/SPL instruction, on-chain account or capsule schema changes. New v0 records with withdrawal-shaped calls use a tagged opening in the existing replay byte field. Older records that need historical lookup tables and did not retain them fail closed; replay never substitutes the current table. Legacy and static-key calls do not need that opening. This is a validator check, not a new L1 authorization requirement.

## What is proved, and what is tested?

The [production helpers](../../token-bridge/src/trace_origin.rs) and [Verus composition](../../token-bridge/proofs/withdrawal_provenance.rs) establish the ten-byte `StartWithdrawal` layout, signer-prefix rule, immediate-parent account containment and its transitivity through arbitrary nesting. For any accepted request/effect row, exact fields and amount come from the resolved call, and a direct owner index is strictly inside the signer prefix. Positive witnesses cover direct and CPI calls. The signature primitive, full Rust replay traversal, address-table deserializer, bank, SHA-256 and SVM execution are **not** formally verified by this suite.

[Bank and journal tests](../../../src/continuity_v2/tests/provenance_tests.rs) cover direct signatures, read-only sources, nested CPI visibility, missing writes, ordered table resolution without a live bank, and durable checkpoint acceptance. [SBF-backed payout tests](../../../src/ephemeral_runtime/tests/token_settlement_v2.rs) cover real direct and CPI withdrawals, repeated requests and restart. To reproduce the pinned Verus run, use suite `provenance` in [the standard runner](INVARIANTS.md#independent-reproduction). See [input hashes and controls](evidence/extended/provenance.json) and [the local run record](evidence/extended/provenance-run.txt).
